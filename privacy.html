Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

YKBI Pilates ("we", "us") is a web app for logging Pilates practice and related training. This policy explains what information we collect when you use the app, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

WhatWhy
Your name and email address, from Google sign-in or the sign-up formTo create your account, sign you in and show your name in the app
A password, if you sign up with email (stored only as a secure hash by our authentication provider)To sign you in
Workouts you log: dates, exercises, apparatus, spring settings, reps, hold times, weights, distances, notesTo show your history, progress charts and personal records
Daily metrics you choose to enter: body weight, sleep, water, calories, protein, notesTo show trends and averages on your dashboard
Your unit preferences (kg/lbs, km/mi)To display your data the way you prefer

When you sign in with Google we receive only your basic profile (name, email address and profile picture link). We do not get access to your Gmail, Drive, contacts or any other Google data.

How we use your information

  • To provide the app's features to you: storing your workouts and metrics and calculating your progress.
  • To keep your account secure.
  • To reply if you contact us.

We do not sell your information, use it for advertising, or share it with third parties for their own purposes.

Where your data is stored

Your account and training data are stored with Supabase, our database and authentication provider. Access rules in the database ensure each account can only read and change its own data. Sign-in with Google is handled by Google. The website itself is hosted on GitHub Pages. These providers process data on our behalf only to run the service.

Your browser stores a sign-in session so you stay logged in. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

Health information

Some data you enter, such as body weight or sleep, may relate to your health. You choose what to enter, it is used only to show your own trends, and it is never shared. YKBI Pilates is not a medical service and does not give medical advice.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your data for as long as your account exists. You can edit or delete any workout in the app at any time. To delete your whole account and all its data, contact us using the support email shown on the Google sign-in screen for YKBI Pilates, and we will delete it within 30 days.

Children

YKBI Pilates is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. Significant changes will be highlighted in the app.

Contact

For questions about this policy or your data, contact the YKBI Pilates team at the support email shown on the Google sign-in screen for YKBI Pilates.
